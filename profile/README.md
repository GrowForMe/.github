## Get Involved

At GrowForMe, we believe technology can transform agriculture, expand access to finance, and create new opportunities for businesses, farmers, and investors across Africa.

We welcome developers, technology enthusiasts, innovators, and partners who share our vision of building a more inclusive and digitally connected agricultural ecosystem.

Whether you're interested in contributing to our technology, collaborating on projects, or exploring opportunities within our ecosystem, there are several ways to get involved.

- [Explore GrowForMe](https://www.growforme.com/)
- [Discover our agricultural and investment opportunities](https://www.growforme.com/)
- [Explore our open-source projects](https://github.com/growforme)
- [Connect with GrowForMe on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/growformeafrica/)

For questions, suggestions, bug reports, or contributions relating to a specific repository, please use that repository's GitHub Issues rather than contacting us through general channels.

Visit [growforme.com](https://www.growforme.com/) to learn more about what we're building.

---

GrowForMe is committed to building technology that is inclusive, transparent, and impactful. We expect everyone participating in our development communities to engage respectfully and constructively.

Our projects adopt a code of conduct that promotes collaboration, respect, and inclusivity. Please refer to the individual repository's `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md` for applicable guidelines.
